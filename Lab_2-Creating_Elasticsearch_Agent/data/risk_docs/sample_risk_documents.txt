GOVERNANCE & RISK DOCUMENTATION
============================================================

RISK ID: RISK-001
Category: Operational Risk — System Availability
Severity: High

Description:
  Critical business systems become unavailable, preventing users from accessing essential applications and services. This includes email systems, ERP, CRM, and other core business applications.

Keywords: system down, unavailable, cannot access, outage, service disruption, application not responding

Related Incidents:
  - Email server down
  - VPN connection failure
  - Database unavailable
  - Application timeout

Mitigation Strategies:
  Implement redundancy, regular backups, failover systems, and monitoring alerts. Establish incident response procedures and communication protocols.

Compliance Requirements: ISO 27001, SOC 2, Business Continuity Planning

RISK ID: RISK-002
Category: Security Risk — Data Breach
Severity: Critical

Description:
  Unauthorized access to sensitive data including customer information, financial records, or intellectual property. May result from phishing, malware, or system vulnerabilities.

Keywords: data breach, unauthorized access, hacked, compromised, suspicious activity, malware, phishing

Related Incidents:
  - Suspicious login attempts
  - Malware detected
  - Phishing email received
  - Account compromised

Mitigation Strategies:
  Implement multi-factor authentication, regular security audits, employee training, encryption, and access controls. Deploy intrusion detection systems.

Compliance Requirements: GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, SOX

RISK ID: RISK-004

Severity: High

Keywords: access denied, permission, unauthorized, cannot open, restricted, forbidden

Related Incidents:
  - Cannot access shared folder
  - Permission denied error
  - Need access to system
  - Account locked

Mitigation Strategies:
